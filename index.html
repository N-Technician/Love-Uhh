Our Story

Born from the passion of Masala Beads, our café sits above a beloved Thamel institution—a rooftop sanctuary that blends Tokyo's Studio Ghibli aesthetic with the warmth of Kathmandu.

Every detail, from Totoro plushies to neon-kissed ceilings, tells a story of nostalgia, creativity, and culinary excellence. We serve not just food, but experiences.
